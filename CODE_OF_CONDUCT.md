# Community standards

Symphone welcomes contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels.

- Be respectful, patient and constructive. Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Respect others’ identities, boundaries and privacy.
- Do not harass, discriminate, threaten, make unwanted sexual advances or publish private information.
- Avoid spam, personal attacks and repeated disruption. Follow reasonable maintainer moderation requests.

These standards apply to the project's issues, pull requests and other project-managed spaces. They do not grant maintainers control over unrelated spaces.

Report concerns to the maintainer through an available private channel. If none is available, request private contact in an issue without naming affected people or publishing sensitive details. Maintainers will seek to respect reporter privacy, but cannot promise absolute confidentiality.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, warn participants, close discussions or temporarily/permanently restrict participation, depending on severity and repetition. Decisions should be proportionate and consider context. Good-faith disagreement is not misconduct.
